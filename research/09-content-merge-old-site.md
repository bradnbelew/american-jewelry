## 4. Voice merge: one register

Old: friendly mall jeweler ("Forever starts here", exclamation points, "favorite"). New: the house (objects as subjects, numbers as adjectives, one honesty line per page). The merge keeps the old site's **facts and warmth** and drops its **sales grammar**.

- Keep: the open studio, while-you-wait, the family, the two towns, the phone numbers, the warranty's generosity ("as often as you'd like, forever" is a great line; say it plainly: "Cleaning and inspection, free, for life").
- Drop: "favorite", "perfect", "unforgettable", "bring your vision to life", "Shop now", exclamation points, "Master".
- Carry one old sentence almost intact because it is true and specific: "Our open air studio is designed so that every customer can watch while their jewelry is being crafted or repaired" → "The bench is on the shop floor. Watch your piece being set."
