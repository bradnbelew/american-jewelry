## Distinctiveness, in two lines
Hide the name: the opener alone (hand, linen, a serif headline) could belong to any contemporary jeweler; the page becomes this house at the green-leather Shooting Star plate and the gold "No. 001, The Cumberland" on navy, with "Brentwood and Mt. Juliet, Tennessee" in the opener label. Forwardable, unhurried: yes, the green-leather plate and the registry are what a person would send; nothing hurries (no popup, banner, counter or "Shop now"), and the page ends on two street addresses.

## The one change that would most raise this page
Make the letter a real Line screen. Set only "We are not the first American jewelry house." alone on the paper at `.h-section` 48px at 1440, move the second sentence to /house, and leave "The whole letter" as the link. The home then ends on one sentence on a full screen, the one romantic mechanic the recipe promises and the page does not yet deliver, and it cuts 26 words (190 to 164), which closes most of the W1 gap and the W2 median while it removes a W6 repeat. The Blocker (T5) is a two-token fix and the first thing to do.
