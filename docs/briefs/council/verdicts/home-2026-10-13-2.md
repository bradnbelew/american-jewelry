## Distinctiveness, in two lines
Hide the name: the opener alone (hand, linen, a serif headline) could belong to any contemporary jeweler; the page becomes this house at the green-leather Shooting Star plate, the gold "No. 001, The Cumberland" on navy and "Brentwood and Mt. Juliet, Tennessee" in the opener label. Forwardable, unhurried: yes, the green-leather plate and the registry are what a person would send; nothing hurries (no popup, banner, counter or "Shop now"), and the page ends on two street addresses.
