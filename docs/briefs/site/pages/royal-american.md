# Royal American: photographic collection

Owner direction, 14 October 2026: replace the numbered registry presentation with an exceptionally clean high-jewelry page. Study Van Cleef & Arpels. Possible 3D exploration is future work; this revision is a finished photographic experience.

This supersedes the older Royal sections in `line.md`, the numbered presentation in the canon, and the previous full-navy-page decision. Existing product identities and one-of-one isolation stay intact.

## Design plan

- Paper `#F6F5F2`, ink `#121212`, secondary ink `#3F3F3F`, muted `#63676E`, photographic navy `#16213A`, focus gold `#B08D57`.
- Source Serif 4 for the collection and piece names; Public Sans for material captions and navigation. Desktop title 56px; mobile 40px. Piece names 28px desktop and 26px mobile.
- Center the short collection introduction. Give the Cumberland one wide photograph, followed by an offset pair of full-object photographs. Left-align each name with its image. Finish with one centered private-viewing invitation.
- All pieces stay visible. Photograph and caption form one link. No selector, numeric markers, price grid, repeated discovery button, or additional visit block.

## Reference and critique

Inspected Van Cleef & Arpels' [High Jewelry](https://www.vancleefarpels.com/us/en/collections/high-jewelry.html) and [Classic High Jewelry](https://www.vancleefarpels.com/us/en/collections/high-jewelry/classic-high-jewelry.html) in the browser on 14 October 2026. Take the centered introduction, photographic authority, generous separation and restrained text. Leave the carousel, uppercase labels, repeated introductions and longer brand prose. No reference images or wording are copied.

The existing navy selector treated the pieces as numbered slides. Merely hiding the numbers would preserve that weakness. This revision makes the jewelry itself the navigation and uses the existing Cumberland landscape and complete necklace/bracelet photographs to determine the composition.

The owner's request for fewer words overrides the old 60–90-word introduction budget (W1). The collection's paper ground is a deliberate C5 update: navy lives in the photography. Mockup photographs and unconfirmed availability are disclosed once. No new inventory, stone facts or working 3D feature are claimed.

Measurement context: the H1 uses the existing house display scale, intentionally larger than the generic reference H1 band (T7/T8), while staying on one line at all measured widths. The gallery contains exactly the three existing Royal concepts: one raster photograph and two SVG viewports over the original photographic contact plate. The raster-only image counter does not count those two photographs. Do not add unrelated inventory or duplicate images to meet a reference image-count range.
