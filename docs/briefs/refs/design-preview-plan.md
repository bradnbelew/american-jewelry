# Four ways to see American Jewelry

Owner direction, October 13, 2026: functional repairs apply throughout; major visual changes stay selectable in a private preview. Lab-grown language is approved. High Jewelry is the discovery label; Royal American remains the line name and URL.

## Reviewable directions

1. **Refined original** — retain the existing photography-led hero, Source Serif / Public Sans and paper/navy palette. Fix the shopping friction without replacing its composition.
2. **The Working Jeweler** — chalk #FAFAF7, graphite #202625, blue #2149B8, brass #9E782F. Public Sans bold for large useful headlines, Source Serif for accounts of design. Hero: large typographic proposition beside an object, followed by a visual design index, a clearly labeled motif study and services. More workshop precision, fewer generic luxury declarations.
3. **In Good Company** — cream #FFF9EF, oxblood #632F38, sky #DCE9F0, ink #342E2B. Source Serif at generous sizes, Public Sans for decisions. Hero: oversized heading above a two-image wearing composition; collections appear before process. More warmth and human scale. Existing images are layout stand-ins, never evidence of a customer or family member.
4. **In Full Light** — white #F7F8FC, ultramarine #183ED0, ink #101D3D, pale blue #E5EBFF. Heavy Public Sans, compact display leading, direct mixed-case labels. Hero: single object on a large bright stage with a functional lighting simulation, followed by a clear lab-grown/natural decision and an aligned product grid. Light modes illustrate interface behavior, not optical performance.

## Shared page system

The selected direction follows navigation through a server-rendered cookie. The picker preserves the current page and its query, supports a shareable URL, is keyboard usable and collapses on phones. Public-live mode ignores it. A private preview desk explains each direction and keeps unresolved evidence visible.

Line pages use direction-specific image/type arrangements with visual product families. Product pages use a compact gallery, visible configuration and explicit size selection. Filters separate stone from production, match price to the selected version, preserve choices and expose individual removals. Cart totals and checkout precede optional packaging information.

## Critique before build

The concepts must differ in hierarchy and content order, not merely accent color. Avoid repeating three identical hero splits. No invented family portraits, drawings, repair histories, testimonials or provenance. Original drawings and real wearing photography are future assets, with explicit slots on the preview desk. No autoplay or pointer-only controls. Keep existing routes, internal variant keys and Shopify cart integration stable. Accessible focus, contrast, reduced motion and phone usability are requirements in every direction.
