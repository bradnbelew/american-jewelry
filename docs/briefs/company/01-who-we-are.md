## The company in five lines (Decided)

American Jewelry is the user's family business: two jewelry stores in Middle Tennessee, family-owned, with an in-house bench.
Since 2026-10-08 it is repositioning from a local family jeweler into an American house of fine jewelry ("the Cartier of America").
The user's brother designs every piece.
Its strongest assets are its reputation (2,537 Google reviews) and its own bench; its biggest leak is local search.
The old site is american.jewelry (Lightspeed); the new domain planned is americanjewelers.com.

## Services (Decided facts from the old site and memory)

- In-house repair: laser welding, retipping, stone setting, resizing, chain and clasp repair, engraving.
- Custom design on CAD/CAM (memory names Matrix Gold): consult, CAD by email, approve, about 4 to 6 weeks.
- Heirloom resets (a family stone into a new setting).
- Appraisals (old price list: from $149 per item).
- Pre-owned Rolex, by appointment only. **Not an authorized Rolex dealer.** Required wording: "pre-owned Rolex", "authenticated by our watchmaker", "not affiliated with or authorized by Rolex" (research/04).
- Watch batteries and service (old price list: battery from $20). Which watch brands stay: **Unknown**.
- Lifetime warranty (old site): defects for life, center stone excluded, wear and abuse excluded, free inspection and cleaning forever. Watches carry the maker's warranty. Final wording needs counsel (Magnuson-Moss, see `06-claims-and-compliance.md`).
- 30-day returns and free shipping (old site). Exclusions for custom, engraved and resized pieces: **Unknown** (draft policy exists).
- Old repair price list (old site, carried into `/services`): simple solder or chain repair from $39; prong retip from $49; small stone replacement from $49; laser engraving from $60; clasp replacement from $69; ring sizing from $69; ring refinish from $75; rhodium plating $85; insurance appraisal from $149; watch battery from $20. Current validity: **Unknown**.
