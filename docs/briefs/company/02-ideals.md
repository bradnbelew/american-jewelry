# Ideals

Purpose: what the house represents, what "American house" and Americana mean and never mean, the values, why customers trust us, and what we will never become.
Last updated: 2026-10-11
Sources: memory american-jewelry-family-business; HANDOFF "What this is" and rules; REMAKE-BLUEPRINT §2; docs/brand-playbook.html (parts one, two, ten); journal "What Designed in Tennessee means"; research/08 thesis.

## The idea in one breath

A house, not a store. Every piece is drawn by our designer, set and finished at our own bench, marked inside, and looked after for life by the same people. (Decided in substance: HANDOFF; wording from brand-playbook, Proposed as a public line.)

## What we represent

- **A hand you can name.** One designer, the user's brother, draws every piece. Nothing licensed, nothing from a vendor catalog. (Decided fact: memory; "nothing licensed" is the target, see `open-questions.md` on vendor inventory still in the cases.)
- **A bench you can watch.** Repair, laser welding, retipping, stone setting and engraving happen in house, where customers can see them. This is the most differentiating true fact we have. (Decided: REMAKE-BLUEPRINT §3. Which store and whether it is behind glass or open: see open questions.)
- **The truth, exactly.** We say what we can prove and stop there. The provenance line is shorter than the ambition on purpose. (Decided: journal "What Designed in Tennessee means")
- **A name that stands behind the piece.** Lifetime warranty on defects, lifetime cleaning and inspection, a mark inside, a certificate. (Decided in substance; warranty terms are draft until counsel reviews, see `06-claims-and-compliance.md`.)
- **Trust already earned.** 2,537 Google reviews across both stores (5.0 Brentwood, 4.9 Mt. Juliet, as of 2026-08-26). The house is built on that reputation, not against it. (Decided fact: memory)

## What "American house" means

- A way of making and speaking: plain sentences, a Caslon, daylight, a street address, a numbered registry, solid weight in the hand. (research/08 thesis, Decided via REMAKE-BLUEPRINT §2)
- Designed in Tennessee by a named person; set and finished at our own bench; standing behind it for life.
- A house in the sense Cartier, Van Cleef & Arpels and Harry Winston are houses: recognizable designs repeated with discipline, not a shop that resells what vendors make.

## What it never means

- Never a flag, an eagle, red-white-and-blue, or a slab of Americana props. "American is a way of making, not a costume." (brand-playbook part ten; REMAKE-BLUEPRINT §2)
- Never "Made in America" or "Made in Tennessee". Our gold and diamonds are imported. (Decided)
- Never "the first American house". Tiffany has used that idea since 1837; Harry Winston and David Yurman are American houses too. (Decided)
- Never "handmade". Pieces are drawn by hand, modeled, cast, then set and finished by hand. Say "hand-set" and "hand-finished" for those operations only. (Decided)
- Never borrowed French or Swiss vocabulary to sound expensive. (Decided)

## Values (Proposed wording; substance Decided)

1. Exact before loud.
2. One subject at a time: one piece, one screen, one sentence.
3. Weight, not show: solid, karat-marked, never hollow, never plated to hit a price.
4. Service is part of the object: the piece comes with the bench, the care and the people.
5. Access, never discount: value is added (engraving, resizing, the box, the care), price is never cut.
6. The family appears by name, or not at all. No invented people, no stock faces.

## Why customers trust us (proof we can show)

- The review record above, shown as one dated line on store pages only. (Decided)
- The bench in sight; most cleaning and inspection on the spot. (Decided fact; location detail open)
- Published repair price list and "quoted under the loupe before anything begins". (storefront services page; prices from the old site, research/09)
- Published stone standards and the IGI or GIA report on every center stone. (Decided)
- The provenance sentence, explained in the journal, with an offer to correct any overclaim the same day. (journal)

## What we will never be

- Never jared.com: no promo bar, no "Shop now", no sale rail, no percent-off, no countdown, no popup, no financing banner, no chat bubble, no 484-SKU bridal grid, no "For Her / For Him". (Decided: REMAKE-BLUEPRINT §2-3)
- Never a DTC man-made diamond discounter: we never sell "a big diamond for less", never show price per carat, never compare a man-made diamond's price or value to a natural one. (Decided: no comparative claims; new-american-tier "The hard truth")
- Never a store that announces it used to be a store: vendor inventory leaves quietly, no public clearance. (brand-playbook part five, **Proposed**)
- Never influencer-led, never emoji, never "we're so excited". (brand-playbook parts six and ten, **Proposed**)

## The test for any decision

"Would a house do this? If the answer is no, we don't." (brand-playbook part ten, Proposed as the house's working test)
