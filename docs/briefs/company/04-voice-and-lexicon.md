# Voice and lexicon

Purpose: how the house writes. Tone, sentence and price grammar, word budgets per page, words to use and never use, compliance phrasing, and before/after rewrites.
Last updated: 2026-10-11
Sources: HANDOFF rules and session-3 item 10; REMAKE-BLUEPRINT §2; research/05 §5b-§5c; research/08 §3, §5b-§5d; research/04 words table; research/09 §4; docs/new-american-tier.html "How we talk about it"; storefront copy.

## Tone (Decided)

Plain, exact, unhurried. A jeweler speaking across a counter to someone they respect. The object is the subject; numbers, names and places are the adjectives. Confident without claiming more than we can prove. Warm through specifics, never through adjectives.

## Sentence grammar

- One idea per sentence, 8 to 14 words. (research/05 rule 12, Decided via HANDOFF register)
- The object is the subject. "We" only when a person acts. (Decided)
- Second person for service ("Bring the piece."), third person for the object ("The star sits on the finger."). (research/05 rule 7)
- Specs in a table, never in prose. Any paragraph over 30 words becomes an accordion, a table or a journal entry. (Decided, REMAKE-BLUEPRINT §2)
- Say each sentence once per site. No sentence that explains the page. No caption that repeats the photo. Delete the second adjective, reassurance and preamble. (research/08 §5c)
- One honesty line per page: "Price before sales tax." "Natural stones vary slightly from the photograph." (Decided)
- Numbers in digits: "3 weeks", "20 characters", "2,537". (research/08 §5c)
- Headings carry no end punctuation unless they are a sentence; taglines end with a full stop. "and", not "&". (research/11 §4, Proposed)
- No exclamation points. No em dashes as separators; use a full stop, a comma or a colon. (Decided)
- Labels and headings in sentence case. Caps only on nav and buttons. (Decided 2026-10-10)
- CTAs name the thing: "Build on Signal", "Every Shooting Star ring", "Book Brentwood". "The design" or "The story" for editorial links. Never "Shop now", "Learn more", "Click here", "Get started". (Decided)

## Price grammar (Decided)

- Fixed price for a fixed piece: "$2,400". Round numbers.
- "From $" only when the stone varies, and only once per piece (not on the card and again on the page; research/08 rule 13).
- "Price on request" only for Royal American.
- "Price before sales tax." under every price.
- Never: "was", "sale", "save", "%", "free", "as low as", "$X/mo", "Regular price", cents.

## Words to use

cast, set, claw, prong, bezel, polish, bench, workshop, jeweler, carat, karat-marked, solid 14k gold, natural diamond, man-made diamond, hand-set, hand-finished, hand-engraved, made to order, one of one, registry, the house, Brentwood, Mt. Juliet, Tennessee, Franklin Road, the designer, first names (once supplied).

## Words we never use (Decided)

- Borrowed vocabulary: Maison, atelier, savoir-faire, bespoke (family ban); métiers d'art, haute (research/05). "Salon" is **Proposed** for removal by research/05 but used in draft copy for Brentwood; see open questions.
- Slop adjectives: stunning, exquisite, elevate, curated, timeless, gorgeous, luxury (as an adjective), premium, finest, perfect, unforgettable.
- Origin: Made in America, Made in USA, Made in Tennessee, American-made, Tennessee-made, built or created in America, #madeintennessee, handmade, hand-made, handcrafted, hand-wrought.
- Stones: lab-grown (as the house term), lab diamond, cultured, synthetic, faux, fake, simulant (for our stones), real, genuine, authentic, precious (for man-made), "New American diamond", grown (alone), above-ground.
- Claims: first American house, sustainable, eco-friendly, ethical, conflict-free (unqualified), investment, holds its value, resale, cheaper, affordable, budget, alternative, GIA-certified.
- Retail register: Shop now, sale, deal, offer, limited time, hurry, favorite jeweler, Forever starts here, Master Jeweler, Jewelry Stylist, For Her, For Him, we're so excited.
- Counting the lines: "three tiers", "three lines", "three doors", "Tier I/II/III", "entry", "lower tier".
- "American" as an adjective outside the line names and the house name (research/08 code 11, Proposed): never "American craftsmanship", "American quality".

## Compliance phrasing (Decided; details in `06-claims-and-compliance.md`)

- Man-made stones: "man-made diamond", hyphenated, immediately before "diamond", same type, every time. Spec line: "Origin: man-made diamond (laboratory-grown). IGI Laboratory Grown Diamond Report No. ____. As grown, no post-growth treatment."
- Natural stones: "natural diamond". Never "real diamond" as a contrast.
- Provenance (exact): "Designed in Tennessee. Set and finished in our workshop. Diamonds and gold are imported."
- Metal: "solid 14k gold", "14k white gold, rhodium-plated".
- Weights: "1.10 ct tw" with the center stone weight stated separately.
- Rolex: "pre-owned Rolex, authenticated by our watchmaker. Not affiliated with or authorized by Rolex."

## Before and after

| Before | After | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "Stunning lab-grown diamond studs, now 20% off!" | "Signal Studs. Two man-made diamonds, 1.00 ct tw, D-F, VVS." | slop word, wrong term, discount, exclamation |
| "Handcrafted in Tennessee by our master jewelers." | "Designed in Tennessee. Set and finished in our workshop. Diamonds and gold are imported." | handmade and origin claim; title inflation |
| "Discover our three tiers of timeless luxury." | "Royal American. Americana. New American." | counts the lines; three banned words |
| "We're so excited to offer bespoke custom design services for every budget!" | "Commission a piece. Drawings within a week, about 4 to 6 weeks to finish." | bespoke, budget, exclamation, no facts |
| "Over 1,000 Five Star Reviews! Middle TN's favorite jeweler." | "2,537 Google reviews between two stores, as of August 2026." (store pages only) | numbers as adjectives, dated, no superlative |
| "SHOP NOW" on a home tile | "Every Shooting Star ring" | CTA names the thing |
| "Real diamonds, not lab." | "Natural diamond, F-G, VS1, GIA report." | never "real" as a contrast |
| "Eco-friendly diamonds grown with clean energy." | "Man-made diamond (laboratory-grown), as grown, IGI report." | no environmental claims |
| "Our elevated New American collection is the affordable way in." | "New American: man-made diamonds to one published standard." | elevate, affordable, ranks the line |
