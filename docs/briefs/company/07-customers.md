## Luxury shopper types (NN/g)

Kate Moran's study names four types; one person can be different types in different categories (https://www.nngroup.com/articles/luxury-user-groups-journeys/):
- **Occasional splurgers**: a durable piece now and then, for an occasion. Our core bridal and anniversary buyer.
- **Big spenders**: frequent, loyal, multi-brand; most luxury revenue. Royal American and the client book.
- **Window shoppers**: fans who cannot buy yet. The journal and Instagram keep them close; never sell at them.
- **Professional stylists**: buy for clients. Locally, stylists for artists (brand-playbook part six, Proposed).

Journey: Discover (social, stores) → **Consider** (online research, the longest phase) → Purchase (often in store) → Use. Brands fail in Consider: poor navigation, too few photos, no scale shots, jargon, missing size and material details, bugs (same source). The site's job is to win Consider and hand off to an appointment.

## Needs by stage

| Stage | What they need | What we give |
|---|---|---|
| Discover | A reason to remember the name | One design, the bench, the dated review line, local search presence |
| Consider | Photos from every angle, scale on a hand, exact specs, plain stone education, price, lead time, returns | Product page spec table, the hand frame, the journal, "From $" pricing, policies in plain words |
| Purchase | A person, a fitting, certainty | Appointment in two clicks, "See it in store", a named human to call |
| Use | Care, sizing, a reason to return | Lifetime cleaning and inspection, resizing, the Family collection's birthstones, the client book |

## Bridal facts (outside, cite when used)

- 52% of engagement rings for 2024 weddings had a lab-grown center stone ("lab-grown" is The Knot's term; ours is man-made diamond); average ring $5,200 (lab about $4,900, mined about $7,600). The Knot 2025 Real Weddings Study via IDEX (https://www.idexonline.com/FullArticle?Id=51079).
- 61% for 2025 weddings; average $4,600 (lab $4,300, natural $7,000). The Knot via Rapaport (https://rapaport.com/news/lab-grown-taking-over-engagement-ring-spending-the-knot/) **[verify]** against The Knot's own page.
- 54% of proposers took 1 to 4 months; the average proposer visited 5 jewelers in 2024; about 7 in 10 said seeing the ring in person mattered. Knot 2024 Jewelry & Engagement Study via Rapaport and JCK (https://www.jckonline.com/editorial-article/10-useful-stats-knots-engagement/). A later survey reports 2 retailers and 64% buying in store; the figures conflict **[verify]** (https://jckonline.com/?p=95186).
- Implication (Proposed): natural diamond is now the minority, premium choice. The house carries both, labeled plainly, and never argues one against the other.

## The market

- Brentwood median household income about $182,088 (DataUSA, 2024; https://datausa.io/profile/geo/brentwood-tn). Williamson County $131,202, the highest in Tennessee (Census QuickFacts, 2019-2023 ACS; https://census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/williamsoncountytennessee/HCN010222).
- Mt. Juliet median household income about $107,068, population about 42,073 (DataUSA, 2024; https://datausa.io/profile/geo/mount-juliet-tn). Wilson County population grew 35.7% from 2014 to 2024 (https://usafacts.org/answers/is-the-population-growing-or-shrinking/county/wilson-county-tn/).

Juliet (https://www.weddingwire.com/biz/kay-jewelers-mt-juliet/3f5ff22b9df955b9.html).
