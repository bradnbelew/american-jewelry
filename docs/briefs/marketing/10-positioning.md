## The position

**Proposed:** American Jewelry is the American house of fine jewelry from Tennessee: designs drawn by one designer, set and finished at our own bench in sight of the customer, and looked after for life by the family that made them.

Short forms (Proposed, from brand-playbook part four): "A house, not a store." "The American house, from Tennessee." "Come and see the bench."

Never: "the first American house" (Decided false), "Made in America" (Decided banned), "Nashville's only..." unless verified.

## Competitive frame

| Set | Who | How they win | How we answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| European and New York houses | Cartier, Van Cleef & Arpels, Harry Winston, Tiffany (Green Hills) | Codes repeated for a century, scarcity, clienteling | The same discipline at a local scale, plus the bench and the family; we never imitate their vocabulary |
| Local independent jewelers | Genesis Diamonds, King Jewelers, Diamond Cellar (Rolex retailer) | Designer brands (Tacori, Verragio), authorized watches, broker pricing | Our own designs, not vendor brands; repair and care as part of the object |
| Mass bridal chains | Signet: Kay, Zales, Jared | Volume, financing, promotions; natural for bridal, lab-grown for fashion (https://rapaport.com/analysis/signets-focus-on-lab-grown-fashion-jewelry-is-paying-off/) | No discounts, no financing banners, no urgency; never jared.com (Decided) |
| Man-made DTC | VRAI ("created diamonds", own zero-emission foundry; https://www.vrai.com/faq), Brilliant Earth ("Beyond Conflict Free" ethics; https://www.brilliantearth.com/news/what-are-brilliant-earth-diamonds/) | Price per carat, ethics and eco stories, online convenience | We never compete on price per carat or environmental claims (Decided). We sell the design, the published standard, the setting by hand and the store |

## What makes the position true (proof points)

Decided facts:
- 2,537 Google reviews; 5.0 Brentwood (995), 4.9 Mt. Juliet (1,542), as of 2026-08-26.
- In-house bench: repair, laser welding, retipping, stone setting, engraving.
- CAD custom work: consult, CAD by email, approve, 4 to 6 weeks.
- Heirloom resets, appraisal, lifetime warranty (terms in draft), lifetime cleaning and inspection.
- Every design by one designer, the user's brother.
- Published stone standards: natural F-G, VS1, GIA; man-made D-F, VVS, IGI.

Proposed, true once built:
- The mark inside every shank, a numbered certificate, the drawing shipped with the piece.
- The bench visible from the floor (store and form Unknown).
- The New American Standard card on the counter, signed.

## The ladder logic (Proposed)

New American is the door most people walk through; it is not the sign over the door. In advertising and windows it gets the smallest share of attention even if it sells the most units (new-american-tier "Guardrails"). Royal American makes the name; Americana is what a family keeps. Never describe this ladder publicly with counts or ranks.

## Luxury strategy anchors (outside)

Kapferer and Bastien's "anti-laws" (secondary paraphrase, https://www.thefashionlaw.com/the-luxury-strategy-the-24-anti-laws-of-marketing/; exact book wording **[verify]**). The ones that fit a local house:
- Luxury is superlative, not comparative: never position by comparison (fits the no-comparison rule).
- Advertising builds the dream, it does not sell (no calls to action in brand creative; Boodles cites this, https://www.marketingweek.com/luxury-brands-in-flux/).
- Don't sell; let clients come. Make access an effort worth making (appointments, private viewings).
- Keep stars out of the advertising.
- Add value, never cut cost or price.
- Raise the average price of the range over time.
