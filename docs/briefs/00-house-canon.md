## What we represent

A house, not a store: drawn by one designer, set and finished at our own bench, marked inside, looked after for life. Exact before loud. American as a way of making and speaking (a Caslon, daylight, a street address, a numbered registry, plain sentences), never a costume: no flags, eagles, bunting. We are not "the first American house" (Tiffany owns that line).

- Hierarchy: line → design motif → piece types → versions, plus Collections that can cross lines.
- Motifs: Shooting Star, Prism (Americana); Signal, Grid (New American). Arch, Honey, Stripes: unknown.

## Hard rules

- Say **lab-grown diamond**, **laboratory-grown diamond** or **man-made diamond**, with the qualifier clearly attached. Lab-grown is approved for discovery and customer wording. Never imply that a laboratory-grown stone is mined, and do not use “real” or “genuine” to disparage either origin.
- NO "Made in America", "Made in USA", "Made in Tennessee" or any variant. NO "handmade", "hand-made", "handcrafted".
- The provenance line, exactly: "Designed in Tennessee. Set and finished in our workshop. Diamonds and gold are imported."
- "Hand-set" and "hand-finished" only for operations confirmed manual.
- No comparative resale, value, price-per-carat or environmental claims for either stone. No "flawless" for VVS. No "GIA-certified".
- Set qualities at set sizes. No stone inventory, no stone browsing. Natural F-G, VS1, GIA. Man-made D-F, VVS1-VVS2, IGI.
- No true scale, no AR, no 3D-printed replica for now.
- The logo is PAUSED. Placeholder mark only (`Mark.tsx`, `icon.svg`). Do not design marks.
- Do not invent facts. Founding year, owners, staff and bench names, hours and financing partner: UNKNOWN. Write TODO and log it. Both locations have customer-visible shops; casting is in-house, but its specific location has not been stated.

## Never do

Discounts, sales, coupons, percent-off, "free" or percent banners, countdowns, urgency, popups, promo bars, financing banners, reviews on product pages, review counts anywhere but one dated line on store pages, carousels, For Her / For Him, value-prop trios, icon rows, bordered-card grammar, chips, badges, line drawings, decorative gradients, hover image swaps, 8-up grids, equal tile rows, repeated intro blocks, promo tiles in a menu, stock or AI imagery (the private mockup may carry the owner's generated comps until the shoot, 2026-10-13; none ships live), model faces who are not the family, text on photos without a real overlay, low-contrast text (muted #63676E is the lightest allowed), anything that shifts when clicked, scroll-jacking or pins longer than one viewport, chat bubbles, "Shop now". Never jared.com.

## Voice in ten lines

1. The object is the subject; numbers, names and places are the adjectives.
2. One idea per sentence, 8 to 14 words. Plain, exact, unhurried.
3. "We" only when a person acts. Second person for service.
4. Specs in a table, never in prose. Over 30 words becomes an accordion, table or journal entry.
5. Say each sentence once per site. One honesty line per page ("Price before sales tax.").
6. CTAs name the thing: "Build on Signal", "Every Shooting Star ring". Never "Shop now".
7. Price: fixed; "From $" once when the stone varies; "Price on request" only for Royal American. Round numbers.
8. Banned: Maison, atelier, savoir-faire, bespoke, stunning, exquisite, elevate, curated, timeless.
9. No exclamation points. No em dashes as separators. Labels and headings in sentence case.
10. Budgets: home 110-150 words; line 60-90; design 120-180; product 90 + 160 folded; builder 60; store 80; House 250-350.

## Visual tokens (storefront/app/house.css)

- Type: Source Serif 4 (one variable file, optical sizes: names and headlines at the large end, the letter, journal and product sentence at the text end; lining figures), Public Sans (UI, numbers, controls). Libre Caslon retired 2026-10-13 (owner: "too scripty"). `.label` is sentence case; caps (`.caps`, 11px, 0.08em) only on nav and buttons. No italics.
- Palette: paper #F6F5F2 (the neutral paper, 2026-10-13), ink #121212, ink-2 #3F3F3F, muted #63676E, hair #DDD8CF, navy #16213A (Royal American and the bag as page grounds; the selected Family tray photograph is the documented photographic exception), gold #B08D57 (hairlines and numerals at 24px+ only). Bone and stone are retired. Photographs sit on the page's own ground (a packshot on white floats with `.float`), bleed to the edge, or sit on navy: no near-miss edges, no tile. Each line's color lives in its photographic ground (navy velvet, deep green leather, pale limestone), never in CSS. White only in control fills.
- Layout: one subject per screen; no two screens of a page share a composition (the screen types: opener, plate, beside, line, ledger, velvet, viewing; `site/22-art-direction.md`); asymmetric 7/5 or 8/4 splits; `--section-y` 12vh / 8vh; hairlines only in tables and lists; photography carries the color.
- Navigation: five destinations (Jewelry, Collections, Bridal, High Jewelry, The House), each opening a full-width panel of clear links with at most one photograph. Search, Visit us, Kept, Bag, Account in the utility row. The phone menu carries the same depth.
- Controls: fixed heights (`.btn` 48px, `.opt` 52px, `.field` 48px), 1px borders in every state, color and border change only; gold focus ring.
- Motion: opacity plus 12px travel, sparingly (not every section); photos settle from 1.02; `--ease-house: cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)`; 300ms crossfade between pages; the ring is the only thing that moves more; full reduced-motion fallback.
- AI-tell lint (research/10): no tracked caps eyebrows, no middle-dot meta strings, no "→" links, no 01/02/03 markers, no fade-up everywhere, no icon cards, no ornaments.
