The AJ mark

Six directions for the house monogram, each tested at the two sizes that matter most: stamped inside a ring, and in gold foil on a navy box. These are rough sketches to choose a direction. A monogram designer draws the final.

The Solitaire

The A is a ring setting seen from the side, with a diamond held in its peak. The right leg sweeps down and around into the J, which becomes the band. It reads as a ring before it reads as letters.

AJ inside a clipped-corner cartouche, like an old assay punch. Made to be struck inside every shank. The quiet, old-house option.

The Single Wire

A and J drawn as one unbroken gold wire, as if bent at the bench: the J rises into the A's crossbar and runs up over the peak. It can be made in gold as a charm, clasp or pendant, so the logo becomes a product.

The Diamond Profile

Turn a brilliant-cut diamond upside down and its side view is an A. The girdle becomes the crossbar, and the J falls from the crown. A detail people in the trade will appreciate.

The Signet Seal

The monogram drawn as the impression a signet ring leaves in wax. Used as a real wax seal on boxes and certificates, and carved into your own signet line. It makes "family jeweler" sound like heritage.

The Hidden Star

The A and J overlap, and a star is cut out where they meet. "American" without a flag. People enjoy spotting it, and it echoes the shooting star design.

Recommendation

Two marks working together

Use The Solitaire or The Signet Seal as the main logo on the site, the storefront and the boxes. Use The Hallmark as the stamp inside every piece. No single drawing works both 4 feet wide on a storefront and 1.5 mm inside a ring, so every great house has a full logo and a punch mark.

The tier system

One mark, three hallmarks

The AJ never changes. A small mark above it says which tier a piece belongs to, so it's visible on the box and inside the ring.

Rules for the final drawing

Must work at 1.5 mm

Engraved inside a band, in one color, with no thin hairlines that fill in when stamped.

No eagles, no flags

Armani Jeans used an AJ with an eagle for years, and Air Jordan is "AJ" too. Patriotic imagery pulls toward souvenir shop.

No mirrored interlock

Two letters woven together symmetrically will look like a Chanel or Gucci knockoff. The A and J should do different jobs.
