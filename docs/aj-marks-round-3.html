AJ, round three

One idea per mark. Drawn as geometry on a 200-unit grid, one stroke weight, no typefaces. Shown at sign size, then at stamp, tag, box and reversed. The sizing row is the real test: a mark that still reads at 16 pixels will survive a 1.5 mm punch.

1

Ascender

The A's right side is drawn straight so it is also the J. One unbroken stroke from the foot, up to the peak, down and under. Nothing to add, nothing to remove.

2

Pendant

The J hangs from the A's crossbar the way a pendant hangs from a chain. Read top to bottom it spells AJ; read as a picture it is a necklace.

3

Stone

A solid A whose counter is a cut stone. The letter is the setting; the empty space is the diamond. A single-letter symbol, the way Tiffany owns a T. The J lives in the wordmark.

4

Cipher

The Ascender device drawn in the high-contrast letter of the old houses: thick stem, hairline leg, a ball at the end of the hook. For heritage over modern.

5

Hallmark

The Ascender struck out of a solid lozenge, the shape French law reserves for a maker's mark. This is the version that gets punched inside every shank and pressed into wax.

6

Diamond

A rhombus, the shape every jeweler's icon starts from. The A's two legs are the diamond's upper edges, its crossbar sits where a true A's does, and the J hangs from it into the pavilion. AJ and a diamond in one outline.

7

Gem

The traditional flat-table gem icon, unchanged, with the girdle doing the A's crossbar's job and the J hanging below it. The A is implied by the crown; the J is explicit. More icon than monogram.

Where I land

Ascender is the AJ I would build the house on: it reads at every size, it stamps, and it has a reason to exist. Stone is the most ownable idea here; it is a symbol rather than initials, and it works as a stud, a clasp and an app icon. Pendant is the wittiest. Cipher is the same idea as Ascender for a family that wants the old-house letter. Hallmark is not a competing logo; it is how whichever mark you choose gets struck into metal. On your question about a diamond shape: Diamond (6) is the one that works, because the letters and the stone share the same lines instead of the letters sitting inside a frame. Gem (7) is there for comparison; it is an icon with a J in it, and the A is a stretch.
