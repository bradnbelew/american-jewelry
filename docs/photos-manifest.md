# Photography manifest

Pulled 25 October 2026 from the family's site, [american.jewelry](https://www.american.jewelry) (Lightspeed). Files live in `storefront/public/photos/`. WebP widths (480, 800, 1200, 1600, 2000) are generated by `storefront/scripts/photos.mjs` into `public/photos/w/`.

Crops remove a corner watermark or a burned-in promotional line. They do not change what the photograph shows. Alts describe the picture, not a new catalog design.
