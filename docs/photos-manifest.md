# Photography manifest

Pulled 25 October 2026 from the family's site, [american.jewelry](https://www.american.jewelry) (Lightspeed). Files live in `storefront/public/photos/`. WebP widths (480, 800, 1200, 1600, 2000) are generated by `storefront/scripts/photos.mjs` into `public/photos/w/`.

Crops remove a corner watermark or a burned-in promotional line. They do not change what the photograph shows. Alts describe the picture.

The mock shows real photographs and planned concept plates together. `PUBLIC_SITE_LIVE` stays unset. When that flag is `true`, any piece without `realPhoto` is unlisted, omitted from the sitemap, and its product page returns 404. High Jewelry is hidden the same way. Status words stay in this file. They are not printed on the site.
